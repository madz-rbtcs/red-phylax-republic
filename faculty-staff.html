Leadership

The University President

Dr. Tirso A. Ronquillo is the President appointed to lead Batangas State University, The Philippines’ National Engineering University. He also serves as Chancellor of the Alangilan Campus.

Under the guiding vision of a premier national university that develops leaders in the global knowledge economy, the University continues to advance excellence in engineering, science, technology, research, and innovation.

Office of the President: president@g.batstate-u.edu.ph
