Admissions

The Batangas State University, The National Engineering University admits all Filipinos, regardless of age, ethnicity, gender, religious beliefs, and social/economic status. Applicants can select preferred degree programs and campuses, but admission remains subject to quota limits.

The Testing and Admission Office (TAO) assists the University in providing quality, excellent, relevant, and responsive education. It is the mandate of the Office to provide effective testing and admission services designed to support and promote the University’s Vision and Mission of academic excellence.

The TAO of the Constituent Campuses (Pablo Borbon, Alangilan, Lipa, JPLPC-Malvar, and ARASOF-Nasugbu) is supervised and monitored by the Assistant Director of TAO - Central.

Further, the TAO provides information services by disseminating information about the program offerings, the examination schedule and requirements, the step-by-step procedure in admission application and other related information; testing services by administering standardized tests appropriate for the clients and prepares psychological test reports of teaching and non-teaching applicants; admission services by evaluating and reviewing the submitted applications, including the checking of requirements as basis for the approval and issuance of the admission test permit and notice of admission to qualified college applicants based on the quota set by the University.

The TAO strengthened its service motto: Leading Innovations, Transforming Lives, Building the Nation; it is also responsive to the University’s sustainability efforts in order to achieve the Global Goals 2030.

Admission Requirements

Applicants must have completed Senior High School in a recognized school or must be enrolled in Senior High School, if not yet completed.

Applicant Type Key Requirements
Incoming Freshmen (Grade 12, SHS) 1 recent passport-size picture with white background (no name tag), electronic/scanned copy of the following:
  • Grades Form 1*, final grades in Grades 10 and 11 in English, Math, and Science.
  • Certified e-copy of Form 137 for JHS and SHS (Grade 11 to 12, or at least Grade 11 for currently enrolled students).
  • Certificate of Completion of SHS with respective strand / Diploma (if graduated), or Certificate of Enrolment with respective strand (if currently enrolled), and copy of e-signature on a white background.
Transferees Transcript of Records / Certificate of Grades (GWA typically 2.0 / 85% with no failing marks in major subjects), Certificate of Good Moral Character, and Transfer Credentials

* Provide a Certificate of Unavailability if grades in priority subjects are not yet available during the time of application. It must be signed by authorized personnel.

Affirmative Action Program

Applicant Type Key Requirements
ALS Graduates Certificate of ALS Rating, Certification of Completion of the ALS Program, Grades Form 2
Indigent Applicants Certificate of Indigency from your respective barangay, Certificate of Tax Exemption from the BIR Office
Indigenous People Certificate of Tribe Membership belonging to Indigenous People / Indigenous Cultural Community
Iskolar ng Bayan Certification of the rank of the applicant in the Top Ten of the graduated (Grade 12) class from the public school. It must be duly signed by the Principal.
Children of Solo Parents Solo Parent ID issued by the Municipality, valid at the time of evaluation. Must be a specific solo parent ID, not any other type of identification card or certificate.
Persons With Disability (PWD) PWD Identification Card (must be valid at the time of evaluation)
Integrated/Laboratory School (Grade 12 Students) The applicant must be a graduating Grade 12 student at the BatStateU Integrated/Laboratory School.

Reminder: The original Grade 12 card of graduated Senior High School applicants must be presented in person when taking the BatStateUCAT for verification. For more details, please visit the BatStateUCAT Portal.
