One University, Many Campuses

Campuses within BatStateU

From a single campus in Batangas City, Batangas State University has expanded into five constituent campuses and seven extension campuses across the province of Batangas. The University is continuously enhancing infrastructure and learning environments across all its campuses to meet the evolving needs of students, faculty, and researchers.
